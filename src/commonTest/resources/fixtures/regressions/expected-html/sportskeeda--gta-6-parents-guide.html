As parents, you should be aware of GTA 6, one of the most popular video games releasing this year. The game revolves around two criminals, Jason and Lucia, and consists of a lot of violence, gore, nudity, drugs, and more. Hence, as a parent, you should know several details about the game before letting your child play it.

Here is a list of important details you should know about GTA 6 as a parent.

7 GTA 6 details to know as parents

1) Ratings

GTA 6 is rated mostly for adults across the globe. Here are all the age ratings throughout the world:

  • US, Canada, and Mexico - ESRB M 17+
  • Europe and other countries - PEGI 18
  • Saudi Arabia and UAE - 21+
  • South Korea - 19+
  • Australia and New Zealand - R 18+
  • India - IARC 18+
  • Japan - CERO Z

These ratings clearly show that the game is only meant for those who are above 18 or are considered adults in their respective countries.

2) Violence and gore level

It is safe to say that GTA 6 will have a lot of violence and gore. According to the now-removed PEGI rating page for the game, decapitation and dismemberment are confirmed to be in the game. Moreover, there is a high degree of realistic violence. Different weapons will also be able to cause different types of decapitation and dismemberment.

The page also states that domestic and wild animals can be harmed and killed by the player controlling the characters. However, the level of detail of the injury will be lower than on human characters.

3) Nudity level

According to the same removed PEGI rating page, GTA 6 is confirmed to have full frontal nudity, strong sexual themes, scenes of intercourse, and more. The game will also feature nightclubs and strip clubs, where the player will be able to pay for private dances.

Additionally, some leaked footage shows areas of the map that will have several naked NPCs that the player can stumble upon.

4) Drug and Alcohol level

For years now, every Grand Theft Auto has allowed players to let characters consume various forms of substances, be it cigarettes, cigars, alcohol, and other strong drugs. The upcoming title will have all the aforementioned substances, including some that have not been featured in any title before.

According to the removed PEGI rating page, Jason and Lucia will be able to carry and actively consume strong drugs right from their inventory. Drinking alcohol and smoking will also be an easily accessible feature.

5) Console Parental Controls

Both PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S offer parental controls that can allow you to limit your child's playtime, or completely prevent them from opening the game.

Here are the steps to access parental controls on PS5:

  1. Open Settings on PS5.
  2. Head to Family and Parental Controls, then to Family Management.
  3. Create the child user's profile and customize specific limits like age restriction, playtime management, and more.
  4. The age restriction will be the key parental control because it will prevent the child from purchasing or opening the game on the console.

Here are the steps to access parental controls on Xbox Series X|S:

  1. Press the Xbox button and go to Settings through Profile & System.
  2. Navigate to Account - Family Settings - Manage Family Members.
  3. Create or select the child's profile to customize their limits, like age and content restrictions, screen time management, and more.

6) No online mode at launch

Some parents might be concerned about their child hopping on multiplayer games without any restrictions and meeting all kinds of people. When it comes to GTA 6, this should not be a concern, at least for now. This is because the game is entirely single-player and will not have any multiplayer modes.

As of now, Rockstar Games has not yet announced a multiplayer mode for the game. But faint rumors have surfaced that it could be released sometime in 2027.

7) No in-game microtransactions

Another concern for parents can be about in-game purchases and microtransactions. Rockstar Games has confirmed that there are no microtransactions in GTA 6. The only in-game purchase players will be able to do is upgrading their game to Ultimate Edition, which unlocks various in-game car shops, salons, outfits, cars, and more.
