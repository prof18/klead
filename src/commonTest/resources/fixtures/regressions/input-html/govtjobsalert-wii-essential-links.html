WII Recruitment 2026: Project Associate, Senior Project Associate, Principal Project Associate & Other Posts

The Wildlife Institute of India (WII), an autonomous institute under the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, wants to hire 12 project staff for six wildlife research projects. The posts run from Administrative Assistant (₹24,000) to Principal Project Associate (₹67,000), all plus HRA. If you studied zoology, ecology, forestry, botany or environmental science, this is a good chance to get field experience at a top institute.

Applications are online only and open from 30 October 2026 to 13 November 2026. Every post is temporary and tied to its project, so read the project length before you apply. Our Other Govt Jobs page has more such openings. Read this article fully on GovtJobsAlert.in so you do not miss a condition.

What Are the Key Highlights of WII Recruitment 2026?

The Recruitment and Placement Cell of WII, in Chandrabani, Dehradun, runs this drive. WII trains people, runs academic courses and advises on wildlife research and management across the country. These jobs support its research projects, so the work is mostly field surveys, data analysis and report writing.

How Many Vacancies Are There in WII Recruitment 2026?

The notification gives no category-wise (UR, SC, ST, OBC) split for any post, so there is no reservation table to show here. Most openings are Project Associate I, so graduates and postgraduates in life sciences have the widest choice.

Each post belongs to one project. The site is named in the project title, and project length differs. A six-month post ends sooner than a one-year post, so weigh that against the pay before you choose.

What is the Eligibility for WII Recruitment 2026?

Educational Qualification

Degrees must be from a recognized institute or university. The “4-year Bachelor’s” in the table means the four-year B.Sc. under the National Education Policy. Experience means R&D work in industrial or science and technology institutions, or scientific activities and services.

For the other science posts, “Natural Sciences” means Wildlife Science, Zoology, Ecology, Environmental Sciences, Geoinformatics or Life Sciences. Botany is added for Nanda Devi. Each post also lists desirable skills such as wildlife field surveys, R modelling, GIS or report writing (for the Administrative Assistant, prior office assistance and team coordination work). These help your shortlisting but are not compulsory. Several Project Associate I posts accept either a four-year Bachelor’s or a Master’s, so a four-year graduate can apply, but the Morand-Ganjal and Nanda Devi posts want a Master’s. Check the row for your own post and project before you fill the form. Browse more post graduate govt jobs and graduate govt jobs on our site.

Age Limit

Relaxation follows the DoPT order of 27 March 2012, as amended. The notification names no minimum age and no “as on” date, so check the cut-off in the form itself. It lists no relaxation for PwBD or ex-servicemen.

What is the Salary for WII Recruitment 2026?

The pay is a fixed monthly amount plus HRA. The notification does not state the HRA rate. For Project Associates, pay depends on whether you have NET or GATE.

If you are close to qualifying NET or GATE, a pass before you apply can lift your pay by ₹7,000 to ₹9,000 a month, depending on the post. Keep the certificate in your upload folder. For the Project Associate II, Senior and Principal posts, a UGC/CSIR/ICAR NET with JRF or WII-NET pass also earns 5 marks in shortlisting. Remember that all these jobs are for the project period only, so treat them as research experience that builds your profile for later PhD or permanent roles.

What is the Selection Process for WII Recruitment 2026?

The institute shortlists on essential qualification, age limit, marks, relevance of your Master’s subject, research experience and publications. Scoring differs by post, so a strong Master’s percentage and proof of related work help you most.

Meeting the minimum qualification does not guarantee an interview call. The shortlist and interview result will be put up on the institute’s news page. Candidates not found suitable for a post may be considered for a lower grade on the interview committee’s recommendation. Use our preparation strategy tips for the interview.

How to Apply for WII Recruitment 2026 Online?

Because you can apply for two posts at most, pick posts that truly match your degree and add the relevance proof for each one: an internship letter, volunteering certificate or work experience letter. Before paying, check that the account holder name reads “RRP CELL REVOLVING FUND / Director Wildlife Institute of India”. Keep your experience certificates ready, because they must show the start and end dates of each job. The application portal opens on 30 October 2026, so prepare scans before then.

What is the Application Fee for WII Recruitment 2026?

A candidate in the exempted group still pays the processing fee, so do not skip it. Pay it through the modes listed in the notification and upload the receipt with the form. The fee is payable for each post you apply for, and it is non-refundable, even if your form is rejected or you withdraw.
