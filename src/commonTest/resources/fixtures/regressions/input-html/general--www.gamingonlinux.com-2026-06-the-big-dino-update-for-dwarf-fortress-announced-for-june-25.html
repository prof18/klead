The big Dino Update for Dwarf Fortress announced for June 25

11 Jun 2026 at 10:24 am UTC
By Liam Squires-Hand
5 comments

Dinos? In my fortress? Oh no. A big update for Dwarf Fortress has been announced to arrive on June 25th featuring a lot of new content.

What to expect from it? The developer said you'll see quite a wide variety of creatures all the way from the Cambrian Explosion to various extinct mammals and birds of the Holocene era. You will need a new world to see the dinos but they said you should see the animal people update in existing saves. Dinos are optional too, so you can tweak your game to your liking. New content includes:

This includes:

  • 100 dinos and extinct creatures, including baby and dinomen versions
  • Dinos can be used for war, as a pet or as food
  • 5 new settings to adjust how dinos appear in your world
  • Animal people graphical update with held equipment and some bodywear
  • ...and more!
