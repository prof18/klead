LUCKNOW: Cyber Crime Police have arrested a 22-year-old man from Dhanbad in Jharkhand for allegedly running a data-driven fraud scheme targeting households with Green Gas connections.

The scam, involving a fake meter-update application, led to losses of nearly Rs 32 lakh, officials said.

The accused, Vivek Pratap Singh, a Class 12 graduate and resident of Balaji Nagar near a law college in Dhanbad, was allegedly involved in the operation for the past six months while also working in the transport sector, police said.

Additional DCP (Cyber Crime) Kiran Yadav said investigators are examining how Green Gas consumer data was collected, who was hired to gather it and how the information was used to identify and approach potential victims.

Singh was arrested from the Dhanbad police station area following a technical investigation and intelligence inputs developed by a Lucknow Cyber Crime Police team, Yadav said.

Police said Singh allegedly hired people on salary to collect household-level information about consumers with Green Gas connections.

The data was then purportedly used to send messages that appeared to be legitimate communication from the gas service provider, persuading consumers to install an Android application package (APK) on the pretext of updating their gas meters.

The case came to light after Lucknow resident Kumud Chandra Agrawal filed a complaint on September 9, stating that he had received an APK via WhatsApp claiming it was required for a Green Gas meter update.

After installation, the application allegedly enabled unauthorised access to his phone and misuse of banking-related information, leading to fraudulent transactions that siphoned off about Rs 32 lakh.

An FIR was registered at the Lucknow Cyber Crime Police Station as Case No. 109/2026.

During the arrest, police recovered Rs 1,23,400 in cash, two mobile phones, six ATM cards, four Airtel Payments Bank kits and 11 SIM cards.

Singh has been brought to Lucknow for further investigation. The seized items are being examined to trace the funds and identify other suspects.
