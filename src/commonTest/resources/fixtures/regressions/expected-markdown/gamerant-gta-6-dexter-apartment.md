***GTA 6*** is full of secrets and easter eggs, with plenty of references to popular [characters from games](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-arthur-morgan-hat-location/), movies, and TV shows. One of these takes inspiration from the Dexter universe, featuring a location that fans of the series should recognize. Dexter Morgan remains one of television’s most iconic characters, with his influence extending into countless discussions and references across popular entertainment.

## Where to Find Dexter Morgan’s Apartment in GTA 6

If you want to check out this easter egg, you will need to drive quite a long distance from your starting location. However, since a large portion of the map is covered in fog, making the trip will also help uncover some of the unexplored areas along the way.

![Where to Find Dexter Morgan’s Apartment in GTA 6 (1)](https://static0.gamerantimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/wm/2026/11/where-to-find-dexter-morgan-s-apartment-in-gta-6-1.jpg?q=49&fit=crop&w=500&dpr=2)

The easter egg is **Dexter Morgan’s Apartment**, located on Washington Island in Vice Beach, northeast of your starting location. Once you reach the area, look for a Pay N Spray shop. Just above it is the apartment building, which should be easy to recognize if you are a fan of the TV series. It is a three-story building with blue doors and blinds on every apartment.

![Where to Find Dexter Morgan’s Apartment in GTA 6 (2)](https://static0.gamerantimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/wm/2026/11/where-to-find-dexter-morgan-s-apartment-in-gta-6-2.jpg?q=49&fit=contain&w=705&h=397&dpr=2)

![Where to Find Dexter Morgan’s Apartment in GTA 6 (3)](https://static0.gamerantimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/wm/2026/11/where-to-find-dexter-morgan-s-apartment-in-gta-6-3.jpg?q=49&fit=contain&w=705&h=397&dpr=2)

Head towards the front entrance, and you will notice that the building is named **Trinity Apartments**. This is a reference to the Trinity Killer, one of the main antagonists who appears in the fourth season of *Dexter*. Unfortunately, you cannot enter any of the apartments or find Dexter’s signature blood slides inside.

![Where to Find Dexter Morgan’s Apartment in GTA 6 (4)](https://static0.gamerantimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/wm/2026/11/where-to-find-dexter-morgan-s-apartment-in-gta-6-4.jpg?q=49&fit=crop&w=500&dpr=2)

There is still something worth checking out, though. Climb the ladder on the side of the building to reach the rooftop, where you will find some equipment lying around. This includes a knife, some rope, and even a needle. These items could reference the tools associated with Dexter’s work as a serial killer, particularly the needles he used on his victims. While you cannot explore the apartment itself, the building and its rooftop details make this a fun easter egg for fans of the series.

With the easter egg uncovered, you can explore more around you or focus on [getting a bank account](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-bank-account-guide/) if you haven't already.
