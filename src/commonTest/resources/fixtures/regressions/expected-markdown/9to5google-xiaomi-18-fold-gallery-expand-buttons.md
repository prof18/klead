The Xiaomi 18 Fold is officially launching next month and, ahead of its formal debut, a hands-on leak is showing off the wider Galaxy Z Fold 8-like form factor.

At an event in China [earlier this week](https://www.gsmarena.com/xiaomi_18_fold_is_officially_coming_in_september_with_the_monstrous_xring_o3_chipset-news-74306.php), Xiaomi confirmed that its Xiaomi 18 Fold would debut sometime in September, powered by the new XRING 03 chipset – the successor to last year’s XRING 01, [Xiaomi’s self-designed smartphone chip](https://9to5google.com/2025/05/15/xiaomi-xring-01-smartphone-chip-reveal/).

Xiaomi mostly teased the chip, which looks to be a bit behind Apple’s A19 Pro, but a big upgrade over the last-generation model.

Far more interesting is the phone, the Xiaomi 18 Fold, which is the latest wide-form-factor foldable.

Hands-on images [posted](https://www.gsmarena.com/xiaomi_18_fold_appears_in_new_images-news-74342.php) to [Weibo](https://weibo.com/7527351868/RfaM9863B?pagetype=profilefeed) provide a better look at the new device. Like the Galaxy Z Fold 8, the overall shape is shorter and wider, opening up to a much wider inner display compared to most foldables, including Xiaomi’s previous releases. This one specifically looks like the [Huawei Pura X View](https://www.gsmarena.com/huawei_continues_pura_x_view_teasers_ahead_of_official_launch_-news-74340.php), even using the same “Dark Cherry” red color that both devices are technically copying from [*rumors* of the iPhone 18 series](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/01/iphone-18-pro-to-offer-only-these-three-colors-per-leaker/) – wild times folks, wild times.
