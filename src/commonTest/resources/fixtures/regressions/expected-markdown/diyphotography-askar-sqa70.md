A [70mm telescope](https://www.diyphotography.net/svbony-sv503-70mm-f-6-78-quadruplet-telescope-launched/) with a short focal length can cover a surprisingly large section of the [night sky](https://www.diyphotography.net/night-sky-photography-sucked-last-night/). This makes it useful for photographing extended nebulae, large star fields, and regions of the [Milky Way](https://www.diyphotography.net/how-to-photograph-the-milky-way-a-beginners-guide-to-astrophotography/). [Askar](https://www.diyphotography.net/askar-sqa106-a-quintuplet-petzval-astrograph-from-askar/) has now updated one of its telescopes in this category. The new SQA70 Pro replaces the original SQA70 with a revised optical design and a slightly faster focal ratio.

The recently announced SQA70 Pro retains the original model’s 70mm [aperture](https://www.diyphotography.net/askar-launches-131f-refractor-large-aperture-flat-field-imaging/) but changes its five-element optical assembly to a six-element Petzval apochromatic design. It combines SD and ED glass and supports full-frame camera sensors. The new model also has a shorter [focal length](https://www.diyphotography.net/focal-length/) of 322mm, compared with 336mm on its predecessor.

## Quintuplet design replaced with six optical elements

The original SQA70 uses five air-spaced optical elements in a Petzval apochromatic arrangement. [Askar](https://www.diyphotography.net/askar-launches-new-refractor-telescope-91f-flat-field/) has added another element to the SQA70 Pro, creating a sextuplet design. The telescope uses one SD glass element and one [ED glass](https://www.diyphotography.net/cant-afford-high-end-glass-use-bokehed-backdrops/) element in the new assembly. Both materials help control [chromatic aberration](https://www.diyphotography.net/chromatic-aberration-prevention/).

![The SQA70 Pro uses ED and SD glasses](https://www.diyphotography.net/askar-sqa70-pro-launched-gets-six-element-optics-and-a-faster-design/Askar%20SQA70%20Pro%20Launched_%20Gets%20Six-Element%20Optics%20and%20a%20Faster%20Design_files/askar-sqa70-pro-1_01kN.webp)

*The SQA70 Pro uses ED and SD glasses*

ED stands for Extra-low Dispersion, while SD refers to Super-low Dispersion glass. Telescope manufacturers use these materials to reduce color errors across the visible spectrum. Askar has combined both types in the SQA70 Pro’s six-element optical system. The new arrangement is a fully air-spaced Petzval APO design.

The Petzval configuration also addresses another common problem in [astrophotography](https://www.diyphotography.net/all-about-astrophotography-introduction-to-photographing-the-night-sky/). A conventional refractor can produce sharp [stars](https://www.diyphotography.net/hubble-captures-stars-moving-inside-an-ancient-star-cluster/) near the center while stretching stars towards the corners. A field flattener corrects this curvature and helps maintain sharpness across the sensor. The SQA70 Pro incorporates this correction into its optical assembly.

## A shorter focal length and full-frame coverage

The SQA70 Pro retains a 70mm aperture but uses a 322mm focal length. The original SQA70 has a 336mm focal length. This 14mm reduction gives the new model a slightly wider [field of view](https://www.diyphotography.net/lenses-an-overview-on-field-of-view-focal-length-and-crop-factors/) when photographers use the same camera.

Askar has also changed the focal ratio from f/4.8 to f/4.6. This makes the new telescope slightly faster than the original. A faster focal ratio helps record extended objects with shorter [exposures](https://www.diyphotography.net/exposure/), assuming the same aperture and imaging conditions.

![It comes with a fixed dew shield and a 290mm dovetail plate](https://www.diyphotography.net/askar-sqa70-pro-launched-gets-six-element-optics-and-a-faster-design/Askar%20SQA70%20Pro%20Launched_%20Gets%20Six-Element%20Optics%20and%20a%20Faster%20Design_files/askar-sqa70-pro-2_01kN.webp)

*It comes with a fixed dew shield and a 290mm dovetail plate*

The SQA70 Pro provides a 44mm image circle, which covers the full-frame sensor format. This allows photographers to use compatible dedicated astronomy cameras, DSLRs, and mirrorless cameras.

Askar provides three rear connection options: M42, M48, and M54. The M48 adapter includes an internal thread for a compatible 2-inch [filter](https://www.diyphotography.net/exploring-the-askar-colour-magic-ultra-e2-duo-narrowband-filter/). This gives users a way to install a filter within the camera connection. It can simplify the imaging train for photographers who regularly use [light-pollution](https://www.diyphotography.net/hoya-announces-new-starscape-filter-to-cut-through-light-pollution-and-a-prond-filter-kit/) or [narrowband filters](https://www.diyphotography.net/svbony-sv240-multi-narrowband-filter-a-solution-for-urban-astrophotography/).

## A compact telescope for portable setups

The SQA70 Pro measures 331mm in length and weighs 3kg as an optical tube assembly. Its weight increases to 3.8kg with the tube rings and dovetail plate. These dimensions make it a relatively compact option for [astrophotographers](https://www.diyphotography.net/astrophotographer-captures-rare-image-of-steve-over-canada/) who travel with their equipment.

A short optical tube can make packing and transport easier. It can also reduce the physical space required on a mount. However, the telescope’s total imaging load will increase once users add a camera, filter wheel, and other accessories.

## Askar SQA70 vs SQA70 Pro: A quick comparison

The Pro version keeps the original telescope’s aperture and [full-frame](https://www.diyphotography.net/why-are-full-frame-cameras-called-full-frame-the-history-behind-the-terminology/) image circle. However, Askar has changed the optical arrangement, focal length, and focal ratio. The new model also weighs slightly more than its predecessor.

The original SQA70 uses a quintuplet Petzval design with two ED glass elements. The SQA70 Pro moves to a sextuplet arrangement with one SD and one ED element. Its focal length drops from 336mm to 322mm. The focal ratio improves from f/4.8 to f/4.6.

| **Specification** | **SQA70** | **SQA70 Pro** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aperture | 70mm | 70mm |
| Focal length | 336mm | 322mm |
| Focal ratio | f/4.8 | f/4.6 |
| Optical design | Quintuplet Petzval | Sextuplet Petzval |
| Special glass | 2 ED elements | 1 SD + 1 ED element |
| Image circle | 44mm | 44mm |
| OTA weight | 2.68kg | 3kg |
| Weight with rings and dovetail | 3.5kg | 3.8kg |
