Let’s give a big round of ghostly boooos!

Our Spooky Showcase Challenge has come to a spooktacular close! In this challenge, we invited our Affiliates to create and submit YouTube Shorts that featured at least three of the seven uncanny add-ons in our [Spooky Spotlight](https://www.minecraft.net/article/marketplace-spotlight-october-2026).\* We wanted to highlight the myriad frights to be found within (and trust us, there are *many*).

On the eve of the Spooky (Spotlight) Season, we received the submissions - and might we say, we were indeed *spooked*. Here are our haunting top picks!\*\*

![A blocky black digital voice recorder with a green screen and a red record button lies on a dark grey surface.](https://www.minecraft.net/content/dam/minecraftnet/games/minecraft/marketplace/Thumbnail_Giro789.jpg)

**Best Storytelling: Giro798 - [Don't BUY FRIENDS on the INTERNET](https://nam06.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fyoutube.com%2Fshorts%2FTTjl9R7YjnY&data=05%7C02%7Cv-katieca%40microsoft.com%7C6bebce62ad154b1a14ba08df33911dfe%7C72f988bf86f141af91ab2d7cd011db47%7C1%7C0%7C639286370521225203%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=XH0tBJ7Ec8XYoerNatZ5AqO9%2BjCpzFefkuhUi%2BzT8RM%3D&reserved=0)**

The apocalypse is better with company… right? See for yourself. For our Best Storytelling award, we were looking for a creator who delivered both an engaging and compelling narrative while showcasing the featured Marketplace content. This short has it all - twists, turns, and one murderous friend.

![A surprised Minecraft character with black hair and a red jacket doesn't see the brown creature with glowing yellow eyes and sharp teeth creeping up behind them in a dark cave.](https://www.minecraft.net/content/dam/minecraftnet/games/minecraft/marketplace/Thumbnail_TheHappyWheels.jpg)

**Best Add-On Showcase: TheHappyWheels1 - [It's Spooky Season On The Minecraft Marketplace!](https://nam06.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fshorts%2FSKBmJrlo6BE&data=05%7C02%7Cv-sanjanasi%40microsoft.com%7C80374e20bc8045dbf7ea08df31550e2b%7C72f988bf86f141af91ab2d7cd011db47%7C1%7C0%7C639283913807537083%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=vMiwrm5XartV4wAECmMB%2FgNeOmbSVJo%2BJUkVcTdA4yI%3D&reserved=0)**

This award went to the short that we felt best showcased gameplay mechanics and features of the included add-ons. In their video, **TheHappyWheels1** takes us on an informative (and ill-advised?) tour of multiple new environments found in three of the featured add-ons. Gorgeous gardens. Scenic subterranean caves. A quaint fishing village. What could go wrong? Well, it’s not about *where* you are, but *what* lurks there with you…

![A tall skeletal creature with glowing red eyes, a skull-like face and long clawed arms stands in a red Nether forest in Minecraft.](https://www.minecraft.net/content/dam/minecraftnet/games/minecraft/marketplace/Thumbnail_Poneiiro.jpg)

**Most Creative Concept: Ponteiiro - [Juntei os 3 Add-Ons MAIS ASSUSTADORES no mesmo mundo!](https://nam06.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fshorts%2FyvgD6DuVYS4&data=05%7C02%7Cv-sanjanasi%40microsoft.com%7C80374e20bc8045dbf7ea08df31550e2b%7C72f988bf86f141af91ab2d7cd011db47%7C1%7C0%7C639283913807551940%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=bb%2BhK2911sqi1tJV%2BbHXlyk%2FW7STqTtzlhrO5Jp5gYQ%3D&reserved=0)**

We loved the creative framing of this short: a confessional session with a doctor, dissecting some *seriously* scary dreams. Surely, that should help soothe the mind…? Well, at least it netted **Ponteiiro** the award for the Most Creative Concept!

How much lingering trauma will you have from playing all these scary add-ons? There’s only one way to find out: get out there and try them for yourself!
