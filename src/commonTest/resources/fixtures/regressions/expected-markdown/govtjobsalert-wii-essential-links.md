## WII Recruitment 2026: Project Associate, Senior Project Associate, Principal Project Associate & Other Posts

The **Wildlife Institute of India (WII)**, an autonomous institute under the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, wants to hire **12 project staff** for six wildlife research projects. The posts run from **Administrative Assistant (₹24,000)** to **Principal Project Associate (₹67,000)**, all plus HRA. If you studied zoology, ecology, forestry, botany or environmental science, this is a good chance to get field experience at a top institute.

Applications are **online only** and open from **30 October 2026** to **13 November 2026**. Every post is temporary and tied to its project, so read the project length before you apply. ### What Are the Key Highlights of WII Recruitment 2026?

The **Recruitment and Placement Cell** of WII, in Chandrabani, Dehradun, runs this drive. WII trains people, runs academic courses and advises on wildlife research and management across the country. These jobs support its research projects, so the work is mostly field surveys, data analysis and report writing.

### How Many Vacancies Are There in WII Recruitment 2026?

The notification gives **no category-wise (UR, SC, ST, OBC) split** for any post, so there is no reservation table to show here. Most openings are **Project Associate I**, so graduates and postgraduates in life sciences have the widest choice.

### What is the Eligibility for WII Recruitment 2026?

#### Educational Qualification

Degrees must be from a **recognized institute or university**. The “4-year Bachelor’s” in the table means the four-year B.Sc. under the National Education Policy. Experience means R&D work in industrial or science and technology institutions, or scientific activities and services.

| Post | Essential Qualification |
| --- | --- |
| Principal Project Associate (Yettinahole) | 4-year Bachelor’s in Natural, Agricultural or Forestry Sciences, or Master’s in Natural or Agricultural Sciences, with 8 years’ experience after that degree; or Doctorate in Science or Engineering and Technology with 4 years’ experience |
| Senior Project Associate (Morand-Ganjal) | Master’s in Natural Sciences or Agricultural Sciences with 4 years’ experience after the Master’s; or Doctorate in Science |
| Project Associate II (RS & GIS) | Master’s in Natural Sciences or Agricultural Sciences with 2 years’ experience after it; or B.E./B.Tech in Computer Science, IT, Remote Sensing, GIS, Data, Electrical, Electronics and Communication, AI or Data Science with 2 years’ R&D experience after it in industrial or science and technology institutions (scientific activities and services do not count on this route) |
| Project Associate I (Morand-Ganjal) | Master’s in Natural Sciences or Agricultural Sciences |
| Project Associate I (Heron project; Yettinahole, 2 posts) | 4-year Bachelor’s in Natural, Agricultural or Forestry Sciences; or Master’s in Natural Sciences or Agricultural Sciences |
| Project Associate I (Vegetation) | 4-year Bachelor’s in Wildlife Science, Zoology, Ecology, Environmental Sciences, Botany or Forestry Sciences; or Master’s in the same or Agricultural Sciences (no Geoinformatics or Life Sciences) |
| Project Associate I (Social) | 4-year Bachelor’s or Master’s in a Social Science discipline: Environmental Management, Social Work, Anthropology, Economics or Sustainable Development Practices |
| Project Associate I (Entomology) | 4-year Bachelor’s in Wildlife Science, Zoology, Ecology, Environmental Sciences, Life Sciences or Forestry Sciences; or Master’s in the same or Agricultural Sciences (no Geoinformatics) |
| Project Associate I (Nanda Devi) | Master’s in Natural Sciences including Botany. Physical fitness is a must for remote Himalayan fieldwork |
| Administrative Assistant (Sijimali) | Graduate degree in any discipline |

For the other science posts, “Natural Sciences” means Wildlife Science, Zoology, Ecology, Environmental Sciences, Geoinformatics or Life Sciences. Botany is added for Nanda Devi. Each post also lists **desirable** skills such as wildlife field surveys, R modelling, GIS or report writing (for the Administrative Assistant, prior office assistance and team coordination work). These help your shortlisting but are not compulsory. Several Project Associate I posts accept either a four-year Bachelor’s or a Master’s, so a four-year graduate can apply, but the Morand-Ganjal and Nanda Devi posts want a Master’s. ### What is the Salary for WII Recruitment 2026?

The pay is a fixed monthly amount plus **HRA**. The notification does not state the HRA rate. For Project Associates, pay depends on whether you have **NET or GATE**.

| Post | Monthly Pay + HRA |
| --- | --- |
| Principal Project Associate | ₹67,000 |
| Senior Project Associate | ₹57,000 |
| Project Associate II | ₹42,000 with NET or GATE; ₹33,000 without |
| Project Associate I | ₹37,000 with NET or GATE; ₹30,000 without |
| Administrative Assistant | ₹24,000 |

If you are close to qualifying NET or GATE, a pass before you apply can lift your pay by **₹7,000 to ₹9,000 a month**, depending on the post. Keep the certificate in your upload folder. For the Project Associate II, Senior and Principal posts, a UGC/CSIR/ICAR NET with JRF or WII-NET pass also earns 5 marks in shortlisting. Remember that all these jobs are for the project period only, so treat them as research experience that builds your profile for later PhD or permanent roles.

### What is the Selection Process for WII Recruitment 2026?

| Stage | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Shortlisting | Marks and relevance scoring from your application, out of 50 |
| Online interview | Top ten shortlisted candidates for each project position |
| Document check | Originals are verified at joining |

The institute shortlists on essential qualification, age limit, marks, relevance of your Master’s subject, research experience and publications. Scoring differs by post, so a strong Master’s percentage and proof of related work help you most.

| Post Group | How the 50 Marks Are Split |
| --- | --- |
| Project Associate I | 25 for Master’s percentage; 25 for relevance to the project (internship, volunteering or past work counts) |
| Project Associate II, Senior and Principal | 5 for NET with JRF or WII-NET; 25 for post-graduation marks; 10 for research or work experience; 10 for publications |
| Field, Lab or Project Assistant category | 25 for Bachelor’s percentage; 25 for relevance to the project |

Meeting the minimum qualification **does not guarantee an interview call**. The shortlist and interview result will be put up on the institute’s news page. Candidates not found suitable for a post may be considered for a **lower grade** on the interview committee’s recommendation. ### Conditions That Can Cost You the Post

| Condition | What It Means for You |
| --- | --- |
| Incomplete application | Missing proof of age, qualification, experience dates, photo, signature or fee receipt makes the form liable to be rejected |
| Fee receipt | Not uploading the fee receipt leads to rejection |

| Earlier WII staff | Those who worked at WII before must upload a No Dues certificate |

| Final say | The Director may reject any application with or without giving a reason, and no inquiry is entertained |
| Temporary job | Posts last only for the project period and end when the project ends |
