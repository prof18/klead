**GTA 6** is now 10 days away from release, but all kinds of fan theories have been thrown around in the community for years now. Ever since the first trailer was released in 2023, GTA enthusiasts have dissected every frame and made initial theories. Over time, more and more speculations and theories started popping up on the internet.

With the game so close to release, let's revisit some of the craziest [GTA 6](https://www.sportskeeda.com/gta/gta-6) fan theories. Some of them might end up being true, while others might be completely wrong.

***Note: If some of these theories are true, they might be considered spoilers. If you do not want any spoilers, do not read ahead.***

## 7 craziest GTA 6 fan theories

### 1) Either Jason or Lucia dies

![There is a very small chance one or both characters die (Image via Rockstar Games)](https://staticg.sportskeeda.com/editor/2026/11/c4989-17942339945601-1920.jpg)

*There is a very small chance one or both characters die (Image via Rockstar Games)*

This is the one theory millions of fans must have thought of at some point but are too scared to talk about out loud. GTA 6 revolves around Jason and [Lucia](https://www.sportskeeda.com/gta/everything-lucia-caminos-revealed-gta-6-extended-look), two criminals who are also lovers. Ever since the first trailer, it was clear that their story is somewhat inspired by Bonnie and Clyde, who eventually die while running away from the law.

Hence, **many fans immediately started thinking that either both, or at least one of the protagonists, might die at the end of the game.** Some even went on to speculate that one might eliminate the other during the climax.

The only way to find out whether this fan theory is true or not is to wait for November 19th and play through their entire story.

### 2) Jason is an undercover cop

![Theory about Jason being a cop (Image via Rockstar Games)](https://staticg.sportskeeda.com/editor/2026/11/28ccf-17942346863499-1920.jpg)

*Theory about Jason being a cop (Image via Rockstar Games)*

Since the first GTA 6 trailer, this fan theory has been doing the rounds on the internet. In the trailer, an NPC resembling [Jason](https://www.sportskeeda.com/gta/everything-jason-duval-revealed-gta-6-extended-look) was standing in front of Mendez, who is a corrupt cop in the game. Furthermore, since Jason's backstory as a military man was revealed by Rockstar Games, **many fans theorized that he could be an undercover cop**, working with Lucia and eventually turning her in.

Despite these connections, this is considered one of the weaker GTA 6 fan theories in the community now.

---

### 3) Jason and Lucia have met each other earlier

![Jason and Lucia might have history (Image via Rockstar Games)](https://staticg.sportskeeda.com/editor/2026/11/7035a-17942354450517-1920.jpg)

*Jason and Lucia might have history (Image via Rockstar Games)*

At first, fans thought that Jason and Lucia were entirely new to each other and that the game would show how they [build their relationship](https://www.sportskeeda.com/gta/rockstar-reveals-jason-lucia-can-break-gta-6). As time went by, however, **several fan theories started popping up speculating that the couple might have a long history, all the way back in Liberty City.**

In the official content provided by Rockstar Games, it is now confirmed that Lucia Caminos was born and brought up in Liberty City. Also, in the [GTA 6 launch trailer](https://www.sportskeeda.com/gta/gta-6-launch-trailer-features-new-original-score-cutscenes), Jason is seen wearing a North Fort Law Training School T-shirt, which was an area in GTA 1 set in Liberty City.

After viewing the trailer, popular GTA YouTuber TGG theorized that Jason and Lucia looked young in the shot provided above, which could mean that it was a snippet of their past. This is why the theory of them knowing each other picked up pace.

### 4) Part of the story could be set in Liberty City

![Liberty City could be a surprise location (Image via Rockstar Games)](https://staticg.sportskeeda.com/editor/2026/11/09e6a-17942360814695-1920.jpg)

*Liberty City could be a surprise location (Image via Rockstar Games)*

Jason and Lucia's history theory gives rise to another speculation that **Liberty City could be in GTA 6**. This is a massive one since adding an entirely new city to the game is not the easiest task.

However, Rockstar Games' previous titles have a pattern of featuring different locations that are not connected to the main map. GTA 5 had North Yankton, while Red Dead Redemption 2 had Guarma. Even CJ briefly visits Liberty City in GTA San Andreas.

The fan theory was further solidified when a GTA 5 leak showed [expanded parts of Liberty City](https://www.sportskeeda.com/gta/news-new-gta-5-leak-reveals-massive-unreleased-liberty-city-dlc) that Rockstar Games was working on.

### 5) Jason has some connection to Arthur Morgan

![Bizarre theory that Jason is somehow connected to Arthur Morgan (Image via Rockstar Games || RDR Fandom Wiki)](https://staticg.sportskeeda.com/editor/2026/11/845c8-17942367242083-1920.jpg)

*Bizarre theory that Jason is somehow connected to Arthur Morgan (Image via Rockstar Games || RDR Fandom Wiki)*

Arthur Morgan is one of the most beloved video game characters of all time. Even though he was a gunslinger back in the late 1800s, several fan theories have popped up, linking him to GTA 6's Jason Duval. **Some fans speculate that Arthur could be Jason's great-grandfather or at least have some family ties**.

The most compelling details that strengthened this theory are the way both look, talk, and behave, especially when both have long hair.

Rockstar Games is known for making these connections in their games and never talking about them, encouraging fans to theorize. If the developers have made some connection, fans will be able to find hints and references in the game.

### 6) A third playable character

![A third secret playable character might be in the game (Image via Rockstar Games)](https://staticg.sportskeeda.com/editor/2026/11/e0f3f-17942392405254-1920.jpg)

*A third secret playable character might be in the game (Image via Rockstar Games)*

From the first trailer, Rockstar Games made it clear that Jason and Lucia are the only two playable characters in GTA 6. However, because of the Red Dead Redemption series, **fans have theorized that the game could have a third secret playable character**, either during the main story or in the epilogue.

In [Red Dead Redemption](https://www.sportskeeda.com/gta/all-red-dead-redemption-2-features-gta-6-extended-look-gameplay), players get to play as Jack Marston in the epilogue after John Marston. In its sequel, we get to play as John Marston in the epilogue after Arthur Morgan. Before the release of these games, Rockstar Games did not even hint that a second character would be playable.

Hence, some fans have theorized that we might be able to play a secret third character, one that Rockstar Games has not yet revealed.

### 7) Full in-game cryptocurrency economy

![Theory that the game will feature a cryptocurrency economy (Image via Rockstar Games)](https://staticg.sportskeeda.com/editor/2026/11/6b9c5-17942400518223-1920.jpg)

*Theory that the game will feature a cryptocurrency economy (Image via Rockstar Games)*

Last but not least, this is one of the more bizarre theories that have come out of the GTA community in the past few years. After seeing the level of detail the game was offering, **one fan theory was that it could feature a real cryptocurrency economy**. It would be a monumental step up from GTA 5's stock market feature, and would allow players to trade real-life cryptocurrency through the game itself.

While this theory might be exciting for those who deal in cryptocurrency, it is highly unlikely that you will be able to trade in the game itself.
