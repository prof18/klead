[Overwatch](https://www.polygon.com/overwatch/) [season 3 starts](https://www.polygon.com/overwatch-season-3-start-time-edt-pdt/) on Tuesday with new Damage hero [Shion](https://www.polygon.com/overwatch-shion-hero-52-gameplay-video/) and a new Hybrid map, Neon Junction, coming to Blizzard's hero shooter. Season 3 will also feature a new battle pass, and new skins themed around the Tokyo-inspired map. Blizzard's newest cosmetics include cat cafe-themed skins and Japanese "street rebel" looks, among others. There's also a new collaboration with Japanese musical duo Yaosobi, which will give Kiriko, Genji, and Hanzo colorful new streetwear looks.

Here's what else *Overwatch* fans can expect in terms of cosmetics for season 3, Into the Tiger's Den.

## Overwatch Season 3 Battle Pass skins

![OVR\_S3\_LegendarySkins\_BattlePass\_001](https://static0.polygonimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ovr_s3_legendaryskins_battlepass_001.jpeg?q=49&fit=crop&w=825&dpr=2)

Season 3's battle pass includes a mix of Japanese-inspired skins and Summer Games-appropriate fits. Check out Freja's new Midsommar skin and the first Legendary for the imposing Shion. Confirmed to get Legendary and Epic skins in season 3 are Freja, Genji, Shion, Lúcio, Cassidy, Sombra, and Junkrat.

## Tokyo Rebel Hanzo Weapon Mythic Skin

Hanzo gets the Mythic weapon skin in season 3, giving his bow a bright purple and blue glow, with spectacular dragon effects.

## Illari Ascendant Phoenix Mythic Skin

![OVR\_S3\_MythicSkins\_Illari\_AscendantPhoenix\_001](https://static0.polygonimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ovr_s3_mythicskins_illari_ascendantphoenix_001.jpeg?q=49&fit=crop&w=825&dpr=2)

Illari is this season's recipient of a Mythic hero skin, which builds on her overall fiery theme.

## Nyan Cafe Ultra Skins

Sierra, Ashe (and BOB), Orisa, Reaper, and Kiriko are working at the cat cafe in season 3, with maid-themed skins for most of the crew. Reaper gets a velvety kitty-themed suit, while BOB gets some big ol' paws.

## Street Rebels Skins

![OVR\_S3\_LegendarySkins\_StreetRebels\_001](https://static0.polygonimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ovr_s3_legendaryskins_streetrebels_001.jpeg?q=49&fit=crop&w=825&dpr=2)

Mizuki, Mercy, Jetpack Cat, Wuyang, and Junker Queen are giving Tokyo delinquent youth vibes with a street rebels theme in season 3. Unfortunately, Street Rebel Junker Queen has already mugged me for however many Overwatch Coins she costs.

## Spellcasters Skins

![OVR\_S3\_LegendarySkins\_Spellcasters\_JPA\_002](https://static0.polygonimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ovr_s3_legendaryskins_spellcasters_jpa_002.jpeg?q=49&fit=crop&w=825&dpr=2)

Wanna see a magic trick? Tracer, Zenyatta, Reinhardt, Lifeweaver, and Venture are all ready to show you some close-up magic with their new spellcasters skins in season 3. You're not invited to their actual play group, though. Sorry.
