The interesting part is that the phone is just as impressive as the savings you can currently snatch. Since it was made to go toe-to-toe against the Galaxy Z Flip 7, Samsung’s flagship clamshell foldable, our friend here delivers exceptional performance—even better compared to the Z Flip 7—thanks to its high-end Snapdragon 8 Elite chipset.

Additionally, its inner 7.0-inch AMOLED screen offers beautiful picture quality, as it has a high 2912 x 1224 resolution as well as HDR and Dolby Vision support. And if that’s not enough, it has a blazing-fast 165Hz refresh rate, making scrolling Insta, reading PhoneArena, and navigating through the Android menus a breeze.

You can’t compete with the best foldable phones if you don’t take gorgeous photos, right? That’s why the Razr Ultra (2025) also rocks a 50MP main camera that takes crystal-clear photos with vivid colors. And when you add a 4,700 mAh battery that can last a whole day without top-ups and 68W charging that can fill it in just 43 minutes, you get a phone that excels in every department—and then some.
